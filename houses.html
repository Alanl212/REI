Four Houses. One flock.

How Houses will work

Your House is your people.

  • Belonging, K–8. Kindergartners and eighth graders in the same House, all the way through.
  • Mentorship. Older students guide younger ones, and everyone has someone looking out for them.
  • Leadership. House captains, House councils, and student voice in decisions.
  • Healthy competition. House points for scholarship, kindness, service, and courage.
  • Celebration. Cheers, colors, traditions, and showcases that make school feel like family.

Daily advisory will connect academic habits, character education, social-emotional learning, goal setting, and community identity.
