Our why

Built with the community. Not brought to it.

Too many students are asked to prepare for tests without being fully prepared to understand their world, communicate their ideas, imagine new solutions, and shape what comes next.

Families across Mobile, Prichard, and surrounding communities have named what they want: high expectations, culturally affirming learning, stronger literacy and math, arts and technology, career exposure, mental-health support, and genuine partnership with families.

Reimagine Education Academy is being designed in response. Built with the community through listening, reflection, design, and ongoing accountability.

The school model

Literacy is the engine.

Literacy and numeracy mastery form the academic bedrock: structured literacy grounded in the Science of Reading, conceptual mathematics that builds reasoning and fluency, regular formative assessment, and timely support through a Multi-Tiered System of Support.

Every student will have an Individual Learning Plan shaped by diagnostic data, student goals, and family input. Teachers will use the 5E model alongside “I do, we do, you do.”

Design element

STREAM+H

Science, Technology, Reading, Engineering, Arts, Mathematics, and Humanities/History, taught as connected ways of understanding and shaping the world. Literacy is the engine, history gives cultural and civic context, and the arts power expression and meaning-making.

Design element

Career exploration from kindergarten

  • K–2Discover: meet community helpers and professionals
  • 3–5Explore: career-connected projects, speakers, field experiences
  • 6–8Experience & plan: pathway projects, mentorship, job shadows, Pre-ACT prep
03 / 05

Design element

SEL + restorative practices

Students will learn to understand themselves, build relationships, regulate emotions, make responsible decisions, and navigate conflict. Daily and weekly social-emotional learning, restorative circles, counseling supports, and a tiered restorative model will help create a safe, affirming, accountable school culture.

  • Restorative circles
  • Counseling supports
  • Belonging
04 / 05

Design element

Community accountability + advocacy

Community voice will remain part of how the school operates, not only how it was founded: family leadership structures, community roundtables, public learning showcases, student advocacy, and regular feedback cycles.

Advocacy is more than speaking up. It is studying issues, listening to others, communicating with evidence, and working toward solutions.

05 / 05

Design element

The K–8 House System

Every student will belong to a cross-grade House that stays with them from kindergarten through eighth grade, for belonging, mentorship, leadership, healthy competition, and celebration. Daily advisory will connect academic habits, character, SEL, goal setting, and community identity.

Enrollment & launch

The road to Fall 2028

Reimagine Education Academy is being designed for a phased Fall 2028 opening, subject to charter authorization. The working plan begins with Kindergarten–Grade 2 and Grade 6, then adds grades over time until the school serves the full K–8 span.

  • Year 1K–2 + Grade 6164
  • Year 2Add Grades 3 & 7276
  • Year 3Add Grades 4 & 8368
  • Year 4Add Grade 5 · full K–8432
  • Year 5Full K–8 model432

Proposed authorized maximum at full operation: 705 students. All enrollment figures are planning targets and remain subject to final application reconciliation, facility capacity, budget approval, Governing Board action, and authorizer approval.

Frequently asked

Good questions

Is Reimagine Education Academy currently open?

No. Reimagine Education Academy is a proposed public charter school in development, with a planned Fall 2028 opening subject to authorization.

Will the school charge tuition?

No. If authorized, Reimagine Education Academy will be a tuition-free public charter school.

Which grades will the Academy serve?

The current plan is to open with Kindergarten through Grade 2 and Grade 6, then add grades through a phased-growth model until the Academy serves the full K–8 span.

Who may attend?

The Academy is being designed as an open-enrollment public charter school. Final enrollment procedures, preferences permitted by law, capacity, and lottery information will be published after authorization.

Where will the school be located?

The school is seeking a facility accessible to families in Downtown Mobile, Prichard, Africatown, and surrounding communities. A final site has not yet been announced.

Does completing an interest form guarantee enrollment?

No. An interest form is used only to share updates and understand community demand. It is not an admissions application and does not guarantee a seat.

How can families get involved now?

Join the mailing list, complete community surveys, attend events and town halls, share feedback, volunteer, and express interest in receiving future enrollment updates.
